Two perspectives

Make it your own

Source-only. Native iOS.

Open the project in Xcode, choose your signing team, and run it on your iPhone. There is no App Store download yet.

Get the source on GitHub ↗
  1. Clone simply-sunny/footsteps and open Footsteps.xcodeproj.
  2. Set your team and a unique bundle ID. Enable Developer Mode on your iPhone.
  3. Run, allow location access, then choose Always and Precise Location in iOS Settings.

Free provisioning expires after 7 days. Unlock once after a reboot before recording can resume. Health access is optional; grant it only when reading steps.
